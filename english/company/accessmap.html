1120 Shimokuzawa, Chuo-ku, Sagamihara, Kanagawa 252-0254, Japan

🚶 By Train

Nearest station: Minami-Hashimoto Station on the JR Sagami Line. It is about a 15-minute walk (1.1 km) from the West Exit.
Directions: From the roundabout outside the West Exit, walk south along the road beside the railway tracks. At Ajisai-dori, turn right without crossing the road. Follow the north side of Ajisai-dori west, cross National Route 129, and continue for about 150 m. The main gate and security office are on your right.

🚗 By Car

From Hachioji or Hashimoto:
From National Route 16, take National Route 129 toward Atsugi at the Hashimoto Gosaro junction. Turn right onto Ajisai-dori at the Minami-Hashimoto 3-chome intersection. Continue west for about 150 m. The main gate and security office are on your right.

From Atsugi or Aikawa:
From Sagamihara-Aikawa Interchange on the Ken-O Expressway, take National Route 129 north toward Hachioji and Hashimoto. Turn left onto Ajisai-dori at the Minami-Hashimoto 3-chome intersection. Continue west for about 150 m. The main gate and security office are on your right.

⚠️ Visiting Us

Japan Smart Energy G.K. is located on the grounds of AESC Japan's Sagamihara Plant. Please check in at the main gate when you arrive.

All visitors must be registered in advance. Please ask your contact at Japan Smart Energy to arrange this before your visit.

🚗 Arriving by Car

After entering the grounds, stop your vehicle and check in at the security office next to the main gate. The staff at the security office will show you where to park and give you directions to your destination.
