1120 Shimokuzawa, Chuo-ku, Sagamihara, Kanagawa 252-0254, Japan

🚶 By Train and on Foot

Nearest station: Approximately 15 minutes on foot (about 1.1 km) from the West Exit of Minami-Hashimoto Station on the JR Sagami Line.
Directions: From the West Exit rotary, proceed south along the road beside the railway tracks. When you reach Ajisai-dori, turn right without crossing the street. Continue west along the north side of Ajisai-dori, cross National Route 129, and proceed for approximately 150 meters. The main gate and security office will be on your right.

🚗 By Car

From Hachioji or Hashimoto via National Route 16 and the Hashimoto Gosaro intersection:
Take National Route 129 toward Atsugi. At the Minami-Hashimoto 3-chome intersection, turn right toward the west onto Ajisai-dori. Continue for approximately 150 meters. The main gate and security office will be on your right.

From Atsugi or Aikawa via the Ken-O Expressway and Sagamihara-Aikawa Interchange:
Travel north on National Route 129 toward Hachioji and Hashimoto. At the Minami-Hashimoto 3-chome intersection, turn left toward the west onto Ajisai-dori. Continue for approximately 150 meters. The main gate and security office will be on your right.

⚠️ Information for Visitors

Japan Smart Energy G.K. is located within the AESC Japan Sagamihara Plant. Please complete the entry procedure at the plant's main gate reception desk when you arrive.

Advance visitor registration is required. Please ask your contact at Japan Smart Energy to arrange your registration before your visit.

🚗 Information for Drivers

After entering the premises, please stop your vehicle and check in at the security office by the main gate. You will then be directed to the visitor parking area and the appropriate route within the facility.
