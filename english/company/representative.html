Message from the Representative

As we work toward a sustainable society, the energy industry is undergoing a historic transformation. Japan Smart Energy G.K. was established to help reduce environmental impact while ensuring a stable energy supply through the adoption of advanced technologies and smart energy solutions.

Our mission goes beyond simply supplying energy. We are committed to providing effective and innovative solutions to the energy challenges faced by local communities and businesses. With passion and responsibility, we will continue building efficient, clean energy networks and helping preserve a healthy planet for future generations.

striving to meet your expectations and contribute to society as a trusted partner. We sincerely appreciate your continued support.

Japan Smart Energy G.K.
Representative Member Seiji Moriwaki
