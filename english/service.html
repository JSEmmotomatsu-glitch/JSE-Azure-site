Business

Electrode Manufacturing for Stationary Energy Storage Systems (ESS)

Based in Sagamihara, Kanagawa Prefecture, we manufacture and sell high-quality positive and negative electrode sheets, key components of stationary energy storage systems (ESS). Through our manufacturing collaboration with AESC Japan, we focus on quality and stable supply as we work to strengthen the foundation of our business.

Smart City & IT Solutions

Drawing on expertise built during our time as Smart City Consulting, we provide consulting and IT system development services for next-generation urban development, smart homes, smart agriculture, logistics, healthcare technology, and related fields. We also provide flexible business support tailored to diverse needs, including digital transformation initiatives and telecommunications infrastructure development.
